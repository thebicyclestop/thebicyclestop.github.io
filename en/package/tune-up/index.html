Tune-Up

A thorough inspection and adjustment to keep your bike safe and road-ready. Perfect for routine care or pre-ride peace of mind.

  • General Inspection
    • Check frame and fork for cracks, dents, or damage.
    • Inspect tires for wear, cuts, and proper inflation (pressure adjusted as needed).
    • Examine saddle, handlebars, and pedals for stability and alignment.
    • Verify all bolts and fasteners are secure.
    • Check hubs for smooth rotation and play.
  • Safety Check & Adjustments
    • Test and adjust brake responsiveness (lever tension and pad alignment).
    • Calibrate gear shifting for smooth transitions.
    • Lubricate chain, derailleurs, and pivot points.
    • Attach a **Safety Check Sticker** with inspection date.
  • Frame & Component Refresh
    • Wipe down frame and components for a clean finish.
    • Provide a handwritten note with findings and care tips.
